## Approach (what this attempt did beyond the round-2 frontier)

The attempt read both rounds' notes, rebuilt the round-2 leader
(warm_00291a90) from its generators, benchmarked every prior engine per size,
and re-derived the machine model with its own microbenchmark suite (mb1–mb9 +
bw.c): ~1.9 zmm vector-uops/cycle sustained issue cap, near-free loads up to
~0.5/FMA, embedded-broadcast and memory-operand FMA at ~1/cycle (avoid),
serializing `vdivpd`/`vsqrtpd`, 28/10 GB/s stream read/RFO-write. Its genuinely
new contribution is **engine C, a 6D-PFA "digit-state" engine for L=36**:
since 36 = 4×9 with gcd 4·9 = 1, CRT factorizes DFT36 = DFT4 ⊗ DFT9 with pure
digit permutations and no twiddles, and because the pointwise map commutes
with any fixed state permutation, the iteration state lives *permanently* on
the 9³×4³ digit grid across all m steps — rows = v-digits (padded stride 72
doubles to kill L1-set aliasing, worth 2×), 64 zmm lanes = u-digits. Phase A
is three shuffle-free vertical DFT9 row passes; phase B does the full DFT4³
register-resident in 16 zmm (lane-parity pair trick with masked-add combines,
plus a fully in-lane u1 butterfly via `vshuff64x2`/`vpermutex2var` with signs
folded into FMAs, ~7% over transpose/DFT/transpose), then fuses the c-add and
a 21-op rsqrt14/rcp14+Newton map into the store pass. It also built and
measured — then honestly rejected — a pencil-prime engine for 13/17/23, a
45 = 5×9 digit engine (3.9 vs 3.55–3.65 ns/el-step, kept only for m ≤ 12
chains where its ~3 ms cheaper per-call setup wins), and a fused one-sweep
L=64 engine-C variant (~35% behind the vendored engine B). The shipped
deliverable is therefore a recombination: engine A for 6/8/13/17/23 (+45 long
chains), the new engine C for 36 (+45 short chains), engine B for 64, plus
allocator tuning (mallopt mmap/trim thresholds, 256 MB heap pre-grow, per-path
warmups). Its own final self-benchmark (NOTES.md): W1 transform wall
0.512–0.525 s quiet vs base.py 5.3–5.4 s (~10.4×) and ~5.2× vs a local
MKL-DFTI harness; engine-C phase rates 2.71/3.76 tsc/el sat at the measured
issue ceiling — "the remaining headroom at 36 is op-count, not scheduling."
The graded numbers above are recorded as-is, not asserted genuine.
